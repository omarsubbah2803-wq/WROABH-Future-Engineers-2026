# Previous Design

## Earlier Concept

During the development of our WRO Future Engineers vehicle, we considered an earlier robot design before selecting the current vehicle.

The earlier concept used a different approach to propulsion and vehicle control from the current design.

## Why We Did Not Use the Previous Design

The previous concept was not selected for the final vehicle because we decided to move to a mechanical drivetrain with a dedicated steering mechanism.

Our current design gives us a clearer separation between:

- Propulsion
- Steering
- Sensing
- Control

The WRO Future Engineers 2026 rules require a four-wheeled vehicle with one driving axle and one steering actuator. They also prohibit a differential-wheeled base and an electronic differential using one motor per side.

For this reason, the final vehicle was developed around a mechanically connected driving system and a dedicated steering mechanism.

## Current Design

The current vehicle uses:

- Four wheels
- Mechanically coupled propulsion
- Dedicated steering mechanism
- Parallel steering
- LEGO Mindstorms EV3 controller
- Pixy2 camera
- Ultrasonic sensor

## Main Difference

The main difference between the previous and current concepts is the mechanical architecture.

The previous concept followed a different propulsion and control approach.

The current design separates propulsion from steering:

- The drive system provides vehicle movement.
- The steering mechanism changes the vehicle's direction.
- The sensors provide information to the control system.
- The EV3 processes the information and controls the vehicle.

## Why the Current Design Was Selected

The current design was selected because it provides a dedicated steering mechanism and a mechanically connected drivetrain that better matches the intended WRO vehicle architecture.

Parallel steering was also selected because its mechanical linkage is straightforward to implement and adjust using the LEGO Technic components available to our team.

## Engineering Decision

The change from the previous concept to the current design was an engineering decision based on the vehicle architecture, competition requirements and the mechanical system available to the team.

The development process will continue to document further changes, tests and improvements.

## Development Status

The current mechanical vehicle has been assembled.

The final software and physical testing results will be added as development continues.
